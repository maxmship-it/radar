- Search a topic under every name people use for it. Visceral fat is also belly fat and cortisol belly. (Nov 17)

- The last page is 10 title and thumbnail combos, each with the real outlier's thumbnail, a title, and one line on his version. No generated images. (Nov 18)
- The chat reply after every board ends with what went well and what did not work. (Nov 18)
- vidIQ stays in use. (Nov 18)
- It must not miss outliers, and it needs checks that prove it. (Nov 20)
- It must be efficient. No wasted searches. (Nov 20)
- YouTube only. Long form only (3 minutes or more). English by default.
- Radar never writes hooks.
- Exposé titles never say "carnivore".
- No invented numbers, studies, videos or URLs. A number Max must check is written [number].
- US spelling. No em dashes. No "not X, but Y" in Radar's own sentences. Titles may use his "You don't need X, you need Y" format.
- The outlier rule: 3x with 100,000 views, or 5x with 50,000 views, or 2x with 300,000 views, on any tool's multiplier or on recent-30. Cross-niche: 10x, 200,000 views, 40 minutes or less.
- Never delete anything. Never create scheduled tasks or reminders. Never generate images, video or voice.
