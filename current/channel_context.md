## The buyer (Built By Biology clients)

Built By Biology is the coaching business the channel feeds. High-ticket fat loss coaching, 90 days, results guarantee. Every video's coaching plug sends people to an application, then a 45-minute call.

Who actually buys, from 31 clients: median age 57. 71% over 50. 71% men, 29% women. Australia 35%, US 32%, UK 10%. 59% have a waist-to-height ratio of 0.6 or worse. 24 of 31 say "belly", "stomach" or "waist" unprompted. 77% have already done keto, 77% carnivore, 65% fasting, 55% calorie counting. 45% are already eating carnivore when they apply.

Three avatars:
- The stalled carnivore, about 55% of clients. Man, mid-50s, did carnivore, it worked then it stopped. Wants the gut gone and his bloods fixed.
- The recomper, about 20%. Man under 45, already normal weight, wants abs. Watches a lot, rarely applies.
- The woman after 50, 29%. Median 61, often normal weight, buying energy, skin, strength, menopause, binge control. Converts at about 2.5x the male rate per view. No video has ever been made for her.

Cross-funnel: under-45s are 53% of views and 23% of clients. 55+ are 26% of views and 58% of clients. Women 14% of views, 29% of clients. Australia 5% of views, 35% of clients.

## What sells and what doesn't

Video tags by application quality: "carnivorefatloss" videos sent applicants at 83% qualified. "carnivorediet" 79%. "losevisceralfat" 66%. Fear-based fat loss titles pull volume and poorer applicants. Stall-based titles pull buyers.

Why calls are lost (31 non-closes): money 53%, partner not on board 17%, nothing in writing 13%, not enough proof 7%, needs time 7%, wants to DIY 3%. Nobody has ever walked because the outcome wasn't exciting enough.

## The color model for suggestions

Every idea gets tagged with the buyer color it sells to. Use these:
- RED wants a result, fast. "Lose 30 lb by March." Buys on outcome and deadline.
- YELLOW wants to look good. "Get shredded." Buys on photos and stories.
- GREEN wants relief and safety. "My stomach. I'm tired. I've tried everything." Buys on the guarantee and sustainability. This is most of the buyers.
- BLUE wants the mechanism. "I did keto and carnivore and stalled, I want to know why." Buys on studies and exact numbers.

The buyers are GREEN and BLUE. The content is BLUE with a fear spine. Ideas that serve GREEN and BLUE convert. Ideas that serve RED and YELLOW get views. Both are wanted; tag which is which.

## Canon the channel holds (every suggestion has to be consistent with it)

Fat must be higher than protein in grams. Insulin is the master lever for fat loss. Dairy is the most common stall he sees in clients. Ruminant meat over monogastric. Walking after meals. Steps over hard cardio. Sleep and cortisol are one of the three master visceral-fat hormones. Fruit and vegetables are man-made and carry plant defence compounds. High LDL on carnivore is not the danger it's claimed to be. Seed oils and ultra-processed food are the villain. Humans are carnivores by biology and by history.

## Voice

US spelling. Short sentences. Deadpan. Present tense for narrative, with character stamps ("This is Nina"). One named person beats a statistic. Studies told as stories: preframe, year, journal, design, exact numbers, both sides, never rounded. One open loop per video, the click is paid off at the end and never in the hook. A genuine concession to the other side in every exposé. Endings cut back to the hook, then the solution, then de-escalate. "Biology always wins" is the sign-off thesis.

No em dashes, colons or semicolons in anything that will be spoken. Never the "not X, but Y" construction. Never "carnivore" in an exposé title.

Hook doctrine: the curiosity gap that made the viewer click must not be answered in the hook. Nearly all the best-performing hooks carry underlying tension, and they feel like they directly affect the viewer's life.
