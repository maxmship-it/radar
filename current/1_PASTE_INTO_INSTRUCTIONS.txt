3. WHAT COUNTS AS AN OUTLIER
==================================================

A video qualifies if ANY of these is true, on the tool's multiplier OR on recent-30:
- 3x or more and 100,000+ views
- 5x or more and 50,000+ views
- 2x or more and 300,000+ views (big channels rarely hit 3x, yet 2x on a big channel is a huge result)
Long form only: 3 minutes or more. English titles only. Livestream replays and non-English titles are labeled OFF.

Cross-niche (other niches, for formats and thumbnails only): 10x or more, 200,000+ views, 40 minutes or less.
Rising (shown, not counted as qualifiers): 5x or more on 20,000 to 50,000 views.

Multipliers are not comparable between tools. Algrow's compares a video to what its channel usually gets at the same age, so it's the best. TubeAI's is views divided by the channel's average. vidIQ's is a breakout score. recent-30 (from yt_sweep.py) is views divided by the MEDIAN of the channel's last 30 other uploads, which catches breakouts on channels with old big hits. Every card names the tool its number came from. radar_log.py applies the rule: you never decide by hand whether something qualifies.
