# How to run Radar

One page. For Max now, and for Brad, Kane or Dylan later.

## Start a session

1. Open Claude Code on the web: https://claude.ai/code
2. Start a new session. In the box where you pick a repository, choose **radar**.
3. In the environment menu next to it (it shows a small cloud icon and a name), choose **radar**. This is the one that lets Radar reach YouTube from a cloud server. Always use it. Never run Radar on your own computer.
4. Type or dictate what you want. Then leave it. A run takes up to 30 minutes.

## What to say

You talk normally. Any of these work:

- "Bacon." or "Everything on seed oils."
- "What's working in fat loss?"
- "Run on this. Here's what I have so far." Then paste draft titles, a script or an outline.
- "You missed this." Then paste the video link.
- "Add this." Then paste the video link.
- "Show me everything on fat loss from the last two weeks." This comes straight from memory. No new searches.
- "Bacon, eggs and seed oils." They run one after another, never at the same time.

## What you get

A PDF, with only this in it:

1. **Last 100 days.** Every on-topic outlier, most views first.
2. **100 days to 18 months.** Same, older videos.
3. **Cross-niche.** Up to 8 cards from other niches whose title format or thumbnail idea transfers. Left out when nothing good turns up.
4. **Last page: 10 title and thumbnail combos.** Your title, the words for your thumbnail, one line on your image, and the real outlier it is modeled on.

Every card has a number in the top left of its thumbnail. The numbers run straight through the whole board.

After the PDF, the chat reply tells you what ran, the numbers, the 3 biggest finds, what went well and what didn't work.

## Giving feedback

Turn your mic on and go through the board by card number:

- "12 is great. 30 isn't relevant. 44 is very relevant."

Radar remembers. A card you call not relevant never comes back on that subject. A card you call great becomes one that every future run must find.

At the end of a run Radar may ask yes or no questions, like "Add this channel to the watch list?" Answer with the numbers: "Yes to 1 and 3." Radar makes the change itself. You never paste anything.

## Good to know

- YouTube is searched from a cloud server, signed out. It never touches your YouTube account or your IP.
- Radar never deletes anything, never posts, never generates images.
- It stops at 30 minutes and hands you what it has, saying what was not done.
- vidIQ use is capped at 100 credits a run. Algrow and TubeAI calls are free and Radar stays under their daily limits.
- If a board says PARTIAL RUN in the top right, a required step failed. The chat reply says which.
