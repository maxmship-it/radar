# PAST CONCLUSIONS: YouTube retention analytics report

Source: chat "YouTube retention analytics report", 1 Nov 2026 (https://claude.ai/chat/ba29224d-5bf8-436c-99c8-c6b4ea78ad61)
Data covers: 43 long-form videos, Studio export 31 Oct 2025 to 31 Oct 2026, plus vidIQ retention
Collected: 23 Nov 2026

Conclusions Claude wrote in an earlier session, copied from the chat. Not re-checked today. The Nov 21 Channel Audit (separate file) uses newer data and supersedes overlapping points.

## Packaging findings
- CTR and average % viewed move in opposite directions (Spearman -0.45). High-click packaging, such as villain exposés and the collab, brings in viewers who watch a smaller share. Lists and myth-busts get fewer clicks and keep viewers longer.
- Higher-CTR packaging still earns more watch time from the same impressions (Spearman +0.52 between CTR and watch minutes per 1,000 impressions). Test & Compare uses that, watch time share, to pick winners.
- How-to videos have the lowest CTR on the channel. Five of the bottom ten by CTR are how-to videos.
- Naming the brand, company or person helped in exposé titles. Outside exposés, naming did nothing.
- No-person thumbnails and the before/after body thumbnail were flagged as weak formats.
- "US beef banned in Europe" (JxdWD25llTU) shows a calm infographic thumbnail today, so its 9.11% CTR may belong partly to an earlier version.

## Upload mix (median CTR by publish period)
- Nov to Jan: 13 videos, 5 exposés, 7.22%
- Feb to Apr: 14 videos, 6 exposés, 8.05%
- May to Jul: 9 videos, 6 exposés, 7.74%
- Aug to Oct: 7 videos, 1 exposé, 7.10%
- Only one of the seven uploads since August was an exposé (Chick-fil-A, NQQ1R05nOoo, 8.49% CTR).

## Testing process (as Max described it then)
- Three thumbnails with the same title, run twice. Then three titles on the winning thumbnail. Then a wider-audience thumbnail swapped in by hand at day 3 to 5.
- Weak point: the manual swap. Judging it by eye on CTR mixes the thumbnail change with the traffic-source shift that moves CTR on its own.
- YouTube warns that early impressions come mostly from viewers who already know the channel.

## Fixes it recommended for packaging
1. Show the villain, critic or affected person on the thumbnail when the story has one, next to Max or on their own.
2. Retest "Vital Farms are finished" with a person on the thumbnail.
3. Replace the before/after body thumbnail with Max's face plus a statement or the main food. Test it on the four videos that use it.
4. Name the brand, company or person in exposé titles. For how-to topics, test a villain or hidden-ingredient angle instead.
5. Keep exposés in a regular slot.
6. Judge every test on watch time share. Use CTR only to diagnose.
7. Run the day 3 to 5 wider-audience thumbnail as its own Test & Compare test against the launch winner instead of a manual swap.
8. After each winning package, check 0:30 retention against typical retention.
9. Keep a dated test log.

## Retention and opening findings
- Vital Farms and "3 Years" scored 0.36 on YouTube's relative retention in the first 10 seconds (0.5 is median).
- A BBB pitch in the last 5% dropped the curve from 0.263 to 0.142, losing 46.1% of remaining viewers. Across 35 videos, pitch endings lost a median 40.5% of remaining viewers, against 13.1% for next-video cards.
- Viewers skipped integrated reads and came back in 16 of 18 reads.
- Rule it gave: lock the title and thumbnail before scripting the intro, then make the first line confirm them.
