# PAST CONCLUSIONS: Who watches against who buys

Collected: 23 Nov 2026. Conclusions Claude wrote in earlier sessions, copied from the chats. Not re-checked today. The fresh numbers are in `../icp/ICP_breakdown.md` and `../studio/`.

## Client demographics and niche analytics breakdown (15 Sep 2026)
Source: https://claude.ai/chat/a958a649-f8c5-44c0-8b0e-c01897941bbb
Data: 31 paying clients' questionnaires, YouTube Studio audience (90 days to mid Sep), BBB sales dashboard (30 days), Typeform May 28 to Sep 14.
- YouTube views (90 days): 85.2% male. Age 13–17 3.2%, 18–24 6.7%, 25–34 20.4%, 35–44 23.2%, 45–54 20.7%, 55–64 15.7%, 65+ 10.0%.
- English-speaking countries are 63% of views. US alone 43%.
- The audience is about 14 years younger than the buyer: median viewer is in the 35 to 44 band, median client is 57. 35 to 44 is the biggest slice of views and the worst-converting adult band. 65+ is the smallest and converts best. 55+ is 26% of views and 58% of clients.
- Australia and NZ were 6% of views and 42% of clients. Countries within two hours of NZ time converted at about 7x everywhere else (calls run on NZ hours).
- Female-facing content has more headroom per view than male-facing content.
- Watch time does not explain who buys. Average view duration sat between 5:47 and 6:44 in every listed country except India (4:38).

## Diet and weight loss business approach (29 Aug 2026)
Source: https://claude.ai/chat/367a055e-aa4c-42f8-96a0-cc75ed044a74
Data: Typeform export, about 210 usable applications, plus channel videos via Algrow.
- Applicants: median age 49, roughly 80% male by first name, mainly US, AU, UK, CA, NZ, professional or retired.
- By UTM source, carnivore mistake and stall content converted to applications at 3 to 10 times the rate of exposé-style videos.
- Applicants skew male, but known paying clients skewed female, so women 50 to 65 may convert at a much higher rate.

## Sales messaging optimization strategy (7 Oct 2026)
Source: https://claude.ai/chat/09a582bb-2215-4fb8-9d88-2bd2304befbd
- Typical buyer: median age 57, women converting at a higher rate than men.
- Content and pitch language skewed younger ("shredded"). Buyers use words like "stalled" and "plateau".
- Strongest content theme for buyers: appetite as the main bottleneck.

## Follower demographics: men over 40 (25 Oct 2026)
Source: https://claude.ai/chat/a4880b61-a491-4a35-9f63-d2e0e028416b
- From a 28-day Studio screen: about 59% of viewers are men over 40 (range 48% to 70%). Assumes the same male share in every age band.

## Audience health problems and nutrition topics (21 Aug 2026)
Source: https://claude.ai/chat/15d1e621-67fa-4b1d-bc70-26fe9279d2b7
- Audience then: 85% male, 57% aged 35 to 64, peaking at 35 to 44.
- Topic framework it gave: felt daily problems (belly fat, energy crashes, poor sleep, joint pain) beat clinical conditions. Format: familiar symptom, then the institution or product responsible, then the fix.
- Symptom-first videos convert buyers better. Product exposés grow the channel and last longer in search.
