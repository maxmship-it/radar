   82% of clients paid within a day of the first call. That call is where the decision gets made.

Script steps that move the close rate
Close rate when each step of the current script happened vs when it did not. Only steps the rep
controls before the prospect has decided are shown.

   What this says
   Setting the agenda and getting a yes, and walking through every past attempt, are the biggest gaps. When
   either was skipped, 1 in 10 became a client. These are early steps the rep controls before anyone knows
   how the call will go.
   Asking what they think is stopping them (+12 points) and giving expert feedback before the pitch (+8)
   both lift the close rate.
   Two steps line up with fewer clients: giving away a specific fix (22% vs 46%) and the cost-of-doing-
   nothing question (32% vs 43%). Both may partly reflect calls that were already cooling, so treat them as
   hints. The new script already says never give the fix.
   Steps that only happen once a prospect is already keen, like asking for the sale or offering a payment split,
   were left out on purpose. They show what the prospect already decided, not what moves them.

The fix list
Eight changes, in order of impact.

   1   Give discovery at least 25 to 30 minutes.
   Under 15 minutes of discovery closed 0 of 11. Walk through every past attempt and ask what they
   think is stopping them.

   2   Set the agenda and get a yes in the first five minutes.
   It's the step with the biggest gap when it's skipped.

   3   Hold the price until after the recap and feedback.
   Price in the first 20 minutes closed at 14%. Price after minute 40 closed at 52%.

   4   Keep the rep under 40% of the talking.
   Turn the three-step pitch into questions and keep it under 3 minutes.

   5   Never give the fix away.
   Name the pattern, then say it's the first thing Max looks at.

   6   Book the follow-up before hanging up, inside four days.
   Most follow-up clients paid within four days of the first call.

   7   Use the new script's partner and decision questions early.
   They're rarely used yet, and partner and "need to think" objections are the most common after money.

   8   Ask which video brought them in.
   Video-led prospects closed at 53%. Knowing the video helps the pitch and tells the content team what
   sells.

   About this data
   98 first sales calls recorded in Fathom from 30 May to 20 Nov 2026. Internal meetings, tests and empty
   recordings were removed. A split recording of one call counts once.
   A client is someone with a Stripe payment matched to their call by name and email, including people who
   paid later without a second recorded call. Two possible matches that could not be confirmed were left out.
   Revenue is net of refunds.
   Each call was scored against the current General Sales Script. Talk share comes from exact word counts
   where possible, and Fathom sometimes mixes up speakers.
   Steps that only happen once a prospect has already decided (asking for the sale, offering a split, a cheaper
   tier) are left out, because they reflect the decision instead of causing it.
   This is a small sample. Treat gaps under 10 points, or groups under 10 calls, as hints.
