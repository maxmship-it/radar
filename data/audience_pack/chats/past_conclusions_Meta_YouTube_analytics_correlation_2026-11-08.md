# PAST CONCLUSIONS: What moves views (correlation across 21 long-form videos)

Source: chat "Meta and YouTube analytics correlation", 8 Nov 2026 (https://claude.ai/chat/a6de03c8-4a5a-4e40-a92e-78000e8c7e59)
Data covers: 21 long-form videos, Studio export to 31 Oct 2026
Collected: 23 Nov 2026

Conclusions Claude wrote in an earlier session, copied from the chat. Not re-checked today. Spearman rank correlation with total views, -1 to +1.

- **CTR: +0.38 across all 21. +0.60 without McDonald's and Visceral Fat.** The strongest controllable link.
- McDonald's (2.1M) and Visceral Fat (1.4M) had average CTR. Visceral Fat had the lowest CTR of all (5.6%). They won on topic reach (15 to 18M impressions), not click rate.
- FDA (11.4% CTR), Beef Banned (9.1%) and 3 Years Carnivore (9.3%) beat similar-topic videos.
- **Subscribers gained per 1,000 views: +0.44 with views, +0.66 with impressions.** YouTube shows a video to more people when viewers go on to subscribe.
- Impressions: +0.93 (near-automatic).
- Video length: +0.31 (weak). Average view duration: +0.18 (weak). Shares per 1k: +0.10. Comments per 1k: -0.08.
- Average % viewed: -0.31. All videos sit at about 25 to 45%. The highest-retention videos (Butter 45%, 72-Hour Fast 40%) were among the smallest. Small core audiences watch longer.
- Later refinement in the same chat: CTR acts as a "gate". It correlates strongly with how fast a video reaches a view threshold but only moderately with final size. Subs per 1,000 views stayed the most consistent independent predictor.
- A head-to-head comparison in the PDF (72-Hour Fast against 3 Years on Carnivore) was found invalid because it used whole-day windows. The Butter Brands comparisons held.
- Caveats given: 21 videos is small. Correlation, not cause.
