# WATCH LIST

Channels to check on every run, one @handle per line, under the bucket they belong to. Radar reads every handle in the run's bucket plus ALL with yt_sweep.py (free, no tool calls). Every channel here is also saved in the Algrow folder "Radar pool", so folder_outliers scans it too. When you add a handle here, Radar also saves it to the pool on its next run.

Radar proposes additions in the coverage log. A proposal needs a channel with 2 or more on-topic qualifiers in a run, or one on-topic miss that the run did not catch. You add the line. Radar never edits this file itself.
